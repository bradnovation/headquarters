limits section especially.*

## 8. Intellectual Property and Work Product
Guidance: state who owns what is created. This is one of the most
frequently mis-negotiated sections; do not guess an industry default.
- Pre-existing IP: [EACH PARTY RETAINS ITS OWN, OR STATE OTHERWISE]
- Work product ownership: [CLIENT OWNS ON PAYMENT / PROVIDER OWNS AND
  LICENSES / OTHER - STATE]
- License grant, if any: [SCOPE, IF OWNERSHIP DOES NOT FULLY TRANSFER]

(IP section: the human-review flag above applies here) [jurisdiction-dependent:
confirm assignment and registration formalities with local counsel]
