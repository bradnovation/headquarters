# CODE OF CONDUCT
<!-- file-class: DOCTRINE -->

This project exists to help people run a chartered staff for their own work.
The same plain courtesy that staff owes its operator is the courtesy this
project's own contributors and participants owe each other.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about the work, not about
  the person who submitted it.
- Keep criticism aimed at the change, the argument, or the draft, never at
  the contributor.
- Ask before assuming intent. A terse comment or a slow review is far more
  often a busy person than a hostile one.
- Disagree openly when you disagree. A quiet grudge helps nobody; a stated
  objection, even a sharp one, can be worked with.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome contact repeated after being
  asked to stop.
- Personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery directed
  at any person or group.
- Publishing someone else's private material, information, or words without
  their consent.
- Sustained disruption of an issue, a pull request, or any other project
  discussion space that prevents others from getting work done there.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project convenes: issues, pull requests, and
discussion threads in this repository, and any other space its maintainer
later designates as project space.

the person raising them.

## Consequences

The response fits the behavior, smallest first: a private word where that
is enough to resolve it, removal or editing of a specific contribution where
the harm sits in its content, and exclusion from the project's spaces where
a pattern continues after being addressed. The maintainer decides which fits
a given case, and the standing instinct is to reach for the least response
that actually stops the behavior, not the harshest one available.
