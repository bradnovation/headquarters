## The charge

Determine what volume of the grocery group's standing order this roastery can
actually hold, and produce the two documents the operator needs in his hands to
answer the buyer: a counter-offer draft and a term sheet skeleton. Commit nothing,
quote nothing, and send nothing.

## Owner

**Operations.** Revenue contributes D3 and General Counsel contributes D4;
neither co-owns the mission. The measurement gates everything else, which is why
the seat that owns the measurement owns the mission.

## Deliverables, as ruled

The five deliverables and their contributors are as set out in `03-plan.md`
section 2. Four of them changed at the ruling, and the changes are the reason
this packet exists rather than a pointer to the plan:

Built against a **one sixty-day cycle** working-capital ceiling written into the model's own text, not only into the ruling record (row 6). ## Sequencing

D4's clause structure starts immediately. D1 runs one calendar week. D2 is built
from D1. D3 is drafted from D2 and queued for the operator. Each deliverable is
gated by the standards-guard as it lands rather than batched at seal.

## Gates that bind this mission

- **G1, spend.** The plan's envelope was 300K to 460K tokens. Dropping the
  two-shift variant on ruling row 7 removes 25K to 40K from the model lane, so
  this mission carries **275K to 420K against the 600K hard cap**. A ruling that
  removes work removes budget with it; leaving the old figure standing would have
  quietly widened the envelope. Every projection lands in `ledgers/SPEND.md`
  before its launch and is reconciled after.
- **G3, external communication.** Nothing in this mission reaches the buyer. D3
  and D4 are drafted, checked, and put in the operator's hands.
- **The standards-guard gates every deliverable**, and the mission does not seal
  without a pass on each. **General Counsel gates D3 and D4** in addition, because
  both carry money and liability, and its output stays propose-only with a
  standing not-professional-advice header, a recommend-human-review flag, and
  governing law left as an unfilled placeholder.

## Non-goals, stated so they are not drifted into

- No renewal-risk view across the other wholesale accounts. That was struck from
  the plan as a second intent and it gets a second meeting.
- No capital purchase. The operator ruled it out in the intent itself.
- No degradation of the storefront's own production, and no change to the
  existing accounts' delivery cadence.
- No approach to the buyer about payment terms. The operator reserved that.

## Resume protocol

`needs-local: no`. D1 is the only phase with a real-world dependency: it needs a
week of actual roasting to have happened, and no session can shorten that. On
resume, read `STATE.md` first, then `FINDINGS.md`, then inventory the disk before
re-running anything. A session that assumes D1 is unfinished and re-measures
wastes a week; a session that assumes it is finished and skips it builds the model
on nothing. Look first.
