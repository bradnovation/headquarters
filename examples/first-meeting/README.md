# Your first Staff Meeting: a worked example
<!-- file-class: DOCTRINE -->

*A complete, fictional Staff Meeting, run end to end by an invented business and
shipped here as documentation. Nothing in this directory is real and nothing in
it is live. Your own `meetings/` and `missions/` directories start empty and stay
empty until you convene something of your own.*

---

## What this is

`org/STAFF_MEETING.md` describes the ritual that turns an intent you hold into
work agents can safely execute. This directory shows that ritual actually run:
every file the protocol calls for, at the path the protocol puts it, in the order
the protocol produces it.

The business is a coffee roastery with a retail storefront and a growing
wholesale line. It is the same fictional business used in `ops/COLD_RESUME.md`
section 5 and in `examples/adding-a-seat/`, kept consistent across the three so
they read as one invented world rather than three. It sells nothing and exists
nowhere.

The decision on the table: a regional grocery group has offered a standing
wholesale order that would roughly double the roastery's output. Taking it
strains capacity the roastery may not have, on payment terms that tie up cash for
two months before a dollar arrives. Take it, take part of it, or walk.

A checklist could have told you the steps. Only a worked example can show the two
things that decide whether the ritual earns its cost: what a real disagreement
between seats looks like when nobody smooths it, and what the adversarial pass
catches when it is genuinely run. Both are here at full size, including the
finding that would otherwise have put a fabricated fact in front of the operator
dressed as a judgment call.

## Reading order

**The fifteen-minute path**, if you want the mechanics and not the texture:
`00-intent.md`, then `02-debate.md`, then `03-plan.md` sections 4 and 6, then
`04-ruling.md`, then findings F-1 and F-2. Everything load-bearing is in those.

**The full read** is the seven steps below, in order, and takes about twice that.

1. `meetings/2026-04-13-wholesale-capacity/00-intent.md` - the operator's own
   words, the constraints he had already settled, who was convened and why, the
   two board reads, and the cost projection filed before any agent launched.
2. `.../01-briefs/` - five briefs, written in parallel, none able to see another.
   Read them in any order; that is what independence means.
3. `.../02-debate.md` - the chair's synthesis. One disagreement is carried forward
   unresolved, on purpose.
4. `.../03-plan.md` - the plan, the envelope, the typed rulings table at
   recommended defaults, and the adversarial pass with each finding shown at the
   place it was patched.
5. `.../04-ruling.md` - the ruling, transcribed verbatim, with the chair's
   interpretation record mapped row by row.
6. `.../05-missions/` - the fan-out record.
7. `missions/wholesale/capacity-and-terms/` - the packet as fanned, `STATE.md`
   walking the full status machine to a seal, and `FINDINGS.md` carrying what the
   work discovered. This is where the live disagreement from step 3 is finally
   settled, by measurement rather than by argument.

## The mechanics this example makes visible

- **`[fact]` versus `[judgment]` typing**, and why a `[fact]` row's default is
  never an answer. Plan rows 5 to 9 against rows 1 to 4.
- **Proceed-on-default**, and the three ways the operator could have ruled. He
  used two of them in one sitting.
- **The pre-ruling adversarial pass**: three skeptics, five findings, two
  blocking. One is the characteristic failure the protocol names by name, a fact
  quietly retyped as a judgment so the plan could keep moving.
- **Gate G1, spend**, as a projection written into `ledgers/SPEND.md` before the
  briefs fleet launched rather than reconstructed afterward, and reconciled after.
- **Gate G3, external communication**, as a plan step that was struck: the draft
  plan proposed sending the counter-offer, and no send path exists here at all.
- **The propose-only counsel rail**: a term sheet with flagged blanks, a standing
  not-professional-advice header, and no jurisdiction assumed.
- **The seal**, gated by the standards-guard on every deliverable, with General
  Counsel joining because money and liability are in play, and the blocked-board
  item posted the day it was discovered rather than held until close.

## What this example is not

It is not a template. The roastery's answer is the roastery's answer, measured
from a business that does not exist. Copy the shape of the ritual and the
discipline of the typing; derive your own figures from your own facts.

It is not a claim about outcomes either. The meeting produced a decision the
operator could defend, reached in about a day, with the reasoning still readable
a year later. That is all any meeting produces.
