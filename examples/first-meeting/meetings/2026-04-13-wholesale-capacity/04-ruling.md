## The operator's ruling, verbatim

> Go on the judgment rows, all four, I don't have a quarrel with any of them and
> the ceiling is the right instinct.
>
> Facts. Yes I want this buyer, concentration and all, I'd rather have one account
> that pays than six that argue. Cash, I can carry one sixty-day cycle and not
> two, so build to that and say it out loud in the model instead of burying it. No
> second shift this year, I don't have the person and I'm not hiring one for an
> account that hasn't proved it pays on time, so don't bother modelling it. No
> exclusivity, not in any form, and I don't want the word in the document even as
> something we're offering to discuss. The sixty days I'll ask them about myself
> once I've seen the model, leave that one alone.

## Chair's interpretation record

correctable rather than buried in the work. Where the ruling is terse, the
interpretation is stated as an interpretation and not as the operator's word.*

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `[judgment]` | Approved at default | Phased ramp with a written ceiling and a named trigger. "The ceiling is the right instinct" is read as endorsing the ceiling specifically, which was the amendment finding A3 put into the row. |
| 2 | `[judgment]` | Approved at default | Model first for anything carrying the number; clause structure in parallel. |
| 3 | `[judgment]` | Approved at default | Term sheet drafted now with flagged blanks. |
| 4 | `[judgment]` | Approved at default | Each deliverable gated as it lands. |
| 5 | `[fact]` | **Answered.** | He wants the buyer. The safe provisional falls away: D3 is prepared as a real counter rather than a contingency draft. It still goes nowhere without him. |
| 6 | `[fact]` | **Answered as a constraint, not a figure.** | "One cycle and not two." No currency figure was supplied and none was asked for. D2 is built to a one-cycle ceiling, and the chair reads "say it out loud" as requiring the ceiling to appear in D2's own text rather than only here. D4's placeholder stays flagged: a contract blank cannot be filled with a constraint. |
| 7 | `[fact]` | **Answered: no.** | The two-shift variant is dropped from D2 entirely, on his explicit "don't bother modelling it." The envelope drops accordingly. |
| 8 | `[fact]` | **Answered: no, and stronger than the default.** | The default was silence plus a flagged open question. The ruling goes further: the word does not appear in D4 at all, including as an offer to discuss, and the flagged note records that exclusivity was ruled out rather than left open. |
| 9 | `[fact]` | **Reserved to the operator.** | He raises payment terms with the buyer himself after seeing the model. D2 keeps both variants and recommends neither, exactly as the safe provisional said. |

**One thing the ruling did not touch:** the offer's shelf life, which the debate's
last pressure test named as a fact nobody in the room could hold. It stays open,
and it is recorded here rather than let pass, because a question raised in debate
and answered nowhere is what a successor session finds three weeks later with no
way to tell whether it was decided or dropped.

## Replication

This ruling is replicated to `DECISIONS.md` as the next numbered entry, dated, in
the same session it was given, before any status flips or any packet fans out. A
ruling recorded in only one of the two places is not fully recorded.

Two parts of it are org-wide rather than mission-scoped, and the ledger entry says
so in its scope line: no exclusivity in any wholesale agreement, and no hiring
against an unproven account. The rest binds this mission only.
