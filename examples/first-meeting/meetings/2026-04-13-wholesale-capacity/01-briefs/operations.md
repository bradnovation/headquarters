# Operations seat brief - WORKED EXAMPLE - fictional
<!-- file-class: DOCTRINE -->

*Worked example. Written independently, without sight of any other brief. This
seat sits in a dual role: convened as a domain seat, and attending by right as a
standing advisory attendee. Section 5 carries the advisory part.*

---

## 1. Position

**Cap the account at partial volume, and measure before promising anything.**

The roaster is rated at thirty-four hours a week of usable roast time. That
number is on the machine and it is not what we have. Real availability is rated
hours minus cleaning between profiles, minus the maintenance window, minus the
storefront's own production, and nobody here has ever counted it honestly. My
working estimate is low twenties, and I am telling you plainly that it is an
estimate.

Against that, the storefront and the existing wholesale accounts already consume
most of the week. Whatever is left is the entire budget this new account can be
paid out of, and I believe the full standing order is larger than what is left.

So my position is not a negotiating stance. It is arithmetic nobody has done.
Measure, commit to a number the measurement supports, and write the ceiling into
the agreement so the ramp cannot walk past it by accident.

## 2. What this seat needs

- One week of measured roast hours, logged by shift, before any volume goes in
  writing. This is the banked innovation-inbox idea from `00-intent.md`.
- The operator's answer on a second shift. It changes the answer completely.

## 3. Risks, including to my own position

- **Against me:** measuring first costs a week, and Revenue is right that an
  opening ask has a shelf life. If the account walks during that week, my caution
  cost the business the account.
- **Against me:** a permanent cap written into an agreement is hard to raise
  later, even when capacity genuinely improves.
- **Against Revenue:** a phased ramp only works if step two has a named trigger.
  Without one it is a promise with no mechanism, and this seat holds the bag.

## 4. Rulings worth asking

| Type | Ask | Recommended default |
|---|---|---|
| `[fact]` | Will the operator run a second roasting shift, and does he have someone to run it? | Safe provisional: model both variants, single shift and two shift, and recommend neither until he answers. |
| `[fact]` | What are the actual usable roast hours per week, after maintenance and retail production? | Safe provisional: measure for one week and flag every hour estimated rather than counted. |
| `[judgment]` | Build the capacity model before drafting terms, or in parallel? | **Before.** The model produces the only number the terms have to carry. |

## 5. Advisory: registers impact

Whatever this meeting rules, three register consequences follow and this seat
carries them: a task row in `registers/TASKS.md` cross-linked to whatever packet
fans out; a wholesale-line risk entry in `registers/PROJECTS.md` naming
concentration explicitly; and, the moment any dependency on the operator appears,
a row on `registers/BLOCKED_ON_OPERATOR.md` posted that day rather than at seal.
