# Finance brief - ADVISORY - WORKED EXAMPLE - fictional
<!-- file-class: DOCTRINE -->

*Worked example. Advisory brief: this seat attends by right, carries no agenda of
its own, and frames its position as constraints on any plan rather than as a plan
for the intent. Written independently, without sight of any other brief.*

---

## 1. Position

**The roaster is not the binding constraint. The cash cycle is.**

Green coffee is bought and paid for before it is roasted. The grocery group pays
sixty days after delivery. Stack those two facts and the roastery funds the whole
ramp out of its own pocket for roughly two months before the first payment lands,
and the ramp is exactly the moment the outlay is largest and the receipts are
smallest. Volume makes that worse before it makes it better.

I am not saying decline. I am saying that whichever volume this meeting lands on,
the number deciding whether it is survivable is not roast hours. It is how much
working capital the operator can tie up across one payment cycle without touching
the storefront's float, and nobody in this room knows that number.

## 2. What this function needs

- His own ceiling, expressed as a figure or at minimum as cycles: one sixty-day
  cycle, or two.
- Every draft carrying a volume commitment carries the cash implication next to
  it, in the same document. A volume number without its consequence is half a
  proposal.

## 3. Risks, including to my own position

- **Against me:** cash caution can talk a business out of the growth that would
  have solved the cash problem. If he can comfortably carry a cycle, this brief
  slowed a good decision down for nothing.
- **Against me:** I reason from the terms as offered. If they are negotiable, my
  constraint moves and this brief overstated it.
- **Against any plan:** an envelope that does not budget its own review and
  closure lanes will come in over projection. That is the ordinary way a
  projection fails, and it is avoidable.

## 4. Rulings worth asking

| Type | Ask | Recommended default |
|---|---|---|
| `[fact]` | How much working capital can be tied up in green coffee across a sixty-day cycle, without touching the storefront's float? | Safe provisional: the model and the terms draft both build against a clearly flagged placeholder. Nothing is quoted, no volume is committed, and no counter is prepared for release until he supplies it. |
| `[judgment]` | Does the cash consequence travel with every volume figure in every draft? | **Yes, in the same document.** Separating them is how a number gets approved without its cost. |
