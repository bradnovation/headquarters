# General Counsel brief - WORKED EXAMPLE - fictional
<!-- file-class: DOCTRINE -->

*Worked example. This function was convened as a domain participant, not only as
a gate, because the intent's substance is contract terms. Written independently,
without sight of any other brief. Nothing this function produces is professional
advice, and nothing it produces leaves the operator's hands.*

---

## 1. Position

**Draft the term sheet now, with the unknowns left as flagged blanks, and treat
three clauses as the ones that decide whether this account is safe.**

A standing order across six stores is not the cafe agreement this roastery
already uses. Three things change:

- **Exclusivity.** Chain buyers routinely ask for it, sometimes in language that
  never uses the word. Any clause restricting who else the roastery may supply,
  in any territory, is a decision about the whole wholesale line rather than
  about this account.
- **Volume commitment against a ceiling.** If a ramp is promised, the agreement
  must say what happens when step two does not arrive: a stated ceiling, a named
  trigger, and a remedy that is not automatic breach.
- **Termination and notice.** An account this size needs enough notice to unwind
  green coffee already bought against it. Terms written for a single cafe do not
  survive here.

Drafting now is cheaper than waiting. A skeleton with three flagged blanks is
filled in an hour once the facts land; one not started is a week.

## 2. What this function needs

- The measured volume, and the ceiling the operator will stand behind.
- His answer on exclusivity before any clause is drafted either way. That is a
  business decision, not a drafting one.

## 3. Risks, including to my own position

- **Against me:** a drafted term sheet looks finished. A hurried reader can treat
  a document with three flagged blanks as ready to send, and this function has no
  way to stop that. The header and the flags are the whole mitigation, and they
  are conventions rather than mechanisms.
- **Against me:** drafting early anchors the negotiation on our own first shape,
  which is not always the shape the facts eventually support.
- **Standing:** this is not legal advice and this function is not a lawyer. Every
  document carries that statement and a recommend-human-review flag, and assumes
  no jurisdiction: governing law stays an unfilled placeholder to be selected
  with a qualified professional.

## 4. Rulings worth asking

| Type | Ask | Recommended default |
|---|---|---|
| `[judgment]` | Draft the term sheet now with placeholders, or wait for the facts? | **Now, with placeholders.** Propose-only, nothing leaves the operator's hands, and blanks are cheaper to fill than the document is to start. |
| `[fact]` | Will he accept any exclusivity restriction, in any territory? | Safe provisional: the draft carries no exclusivity clause at all, plus a flagged note that the question is open and unanswered. |
