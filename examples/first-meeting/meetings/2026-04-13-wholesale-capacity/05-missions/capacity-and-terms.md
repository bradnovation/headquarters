## Example economy, stated plainly

In a real repository the full packet text sits in **both** places: here, as the
record of what was fanned, and at `missions/wholesale/capacity-and-terms/`, as
the charge the executing seat reads. The two copies are identical.

This example carries the text once, at the mission path, and leaves this file as
a pointer. That is a shortcut for brevity, named here rather than left to be
noticed. Do not copy it: the duplicate is the point, because a meeting directory
that cannot be read end to end without opening another tree has lost the property
that makes the ritual worth its cost.
