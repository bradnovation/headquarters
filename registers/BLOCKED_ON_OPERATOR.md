# BLOCKED_ON_OPERATOR - items only you can unblock

*Owned by the Operations seat (`org/seats/operations/CHARTER.md`); surfaced by
the Chief of Staff at the top of every Staff Meeting. Append-only: one H2
dated section per item, newest on top. Cross-reference `registers/TASKS.md`
for the fuller task context each item is drawn from.*

*What belongs here: an act that requires your own hand and nothing else - a
ruling only you can make, a send or a deploy that structurally requires you
because no send or deploy path exists for any agent (gates G3 and G4), a
document you need to read before anything downstream can move. What does not
belong here: work a seat can simply go do on its own. If an item sits on this
board for a long stretch without anyone checking whether it still needs you,
that is itself a finding for the next Operations sweep.*

*A distinction worth keeping sharp as this register fills: some items **ask**
you for something - your reading time, a ruling, a decision between options
already laid out. Those can wait in this queue at no real cost. Other items
**supply** you with something already finished and ready to act on - a draft
ready to send, a deploy waiting on your word, a package ready to hand to
someone. Items in the second category decay the longer they sit, because the
work behind them is already done and is now simply waiting on you. Do not let
a freshness sweep quietly file a "supplies you" item next to an "asks you"
item as if they carried the same urgency just because both are open rows on
the same page.*

*Status values: **open** (still waiting on you) - **resolved** (you acted;
say what happened) - **superseded** (events overtook it before you acted; say
what changed). Close an item by appending a new dated section that references
it, never by editing or deleting the original.*
